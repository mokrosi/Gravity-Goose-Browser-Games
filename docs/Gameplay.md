# GAMEPLAY

## Character Moveset (Bloop)
1. **Variable Jump**: Hold jump for maximum height. Release early to short hop.
2. **Jump Buffering**: Inputs are saved 120ms before landing.
3. **Coyote Time**: 120ms window to jump after leaving a ledge.
4. **Wall Stick**: Touching a wall mid-air sticks Bloop for 300ms before sliding down.
5. **Wall Jump**: Pushes Bloop away and up from the wall.
6. **Splat Dash**: Instant horizontal speed burst that ignores gravity. 
7. **Ground Pound**: Heavy vertical drop. Bouncing on enemies out of a ground pound results in an extreme bounce.

## Enemy Interactions
- **Intern**: Runs away when you approach.
- **Manager**: Slowly chases you. Dashing into them kills them comically.
- **Drone**: Sine-wave patrol. Can be used as a stepping stone.
